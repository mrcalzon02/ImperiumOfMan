IMPERIUM REFORGED 1.1.9 — OCCULT CAPABILITIES AND ORGANIC RECYCLING

Research and recipe knowledge

Occult Research has six purchases: Culture, Distillation, Manifestation,
Binding, Infusion and Soulforge. They grant fabrication capability. They do
not teach recipes, refresh recipe knowledge on load, or grant a recipe when
Production is opened. Each level after the first requires the previous level.

Physical pattern chips provide recipe knowledge through existing faction
stock and contract-reward routes. Each chip teaches one selected recipe from
its small pool. The source declares 21 pools and 53 candidate new formulas;
existing native formulas and missing native records are skipped before the
runtime chip pools are created. An order needs both its learned recipe and
its purchased research capability.

Existing saves that contain the old facility purchases receive a one-time
capability migration. Old Manifestation, Binding, Infusion and Soulforge
progress maps to the corresponding new level, including its prerequisites.
Only the four obsolete facility markers are retired. Learned recipes, other
purchases and cargo are unchanged; saves without old markers are untouched.

Production

The starter chain uses final quantities, without the ordinary helper's
input reduction or batch multiplier:

  Clone gestation:      no cargo       -> 2 Raw Clones          / 8 hours
  Torment containment:  2 Raw Clones   -> 4 Torment Imprints     / 4 hours
  Suffering separation:4 Imprints     -> 4 Suffering Essence    / 4 hours
  Gavvakh condensation:4 Essence      -> 2 native Gavvakh       / 4 hours

Culture enables the first two processes; Distillation enables the last two.
The native Gavvakh formula takes precedence if one already exists. Production
speed/difficulty modifiers and normal production-line scheduling still apply.

Selected clone recycling instead spends one Raw Clone for four flesh, two
skin and one rib, when those installed native records exist. It does not
spawn an actor or award combat rewards. A clone spent this way cannot also
enter torment production.

Native spoiled organics are detected from their actual expiry mappings, with
a bounded rotten-flesh fallback. Their selected ship-recycling route returns
two Raw Vat Biomass per item. Fresh-food and native antibiotic routes remain
available. Ordinary food, dressings, medicines and mechanical stock retain
normal crafting access.

Dirty Corpse Starch can now be recycled in batches of eight for one Raw Vat
Biomass. Selected stacks count together. Incomplete batches are retained,
and the original leftover objects keep their decay and item state without
merging. Locked and quest custom-profile inputs are protected from both the
custom pass and the native recycler. Ordinary native-only behavior is retained.
The existing recycle button shows the exact return and remainder; a partial
ration-only selection stays visible but disabled, with an explanation.

Presentation

Six generated research images have matched green normal and crimson locked
versions. The game controls available/purchased opacity and its yellow markers.
The clone, imprint, essence and physical pattern chip each have inventory,
floor and shadow sprites: 24 runtime PNGs in total. Source artwork and the
repeatable export script are included in the repository.

The Occult Research ship pip is positioned between the native Engineering
and Research anchors. Research tooltips explain capabilities and acquired
recipe knowledge. A learned recipe whose capability is missing is dimmed in
Production and explains the required research instead of starting an order.

The Crafting Map reports knowledge, capability and materials separately. The
offline source map now includes the occult candidates with exact quantities
and physical chip sources. The six-capability reference replaces the original
four-facility proposal wherever that old design differs from current source.

Preserved acquisition rules

Fury Sigil remains purchase-only. Shadowblade remains a finale reward.
Purity Seal reward behavior is retained. Existing native formulas and the
player's already learned recipes are preserved.
