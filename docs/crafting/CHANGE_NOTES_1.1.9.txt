[h1]In-game Crafting map[/h1]
[list]
[*]Added a Crafting map tab on the left edge of Production and its recipe picker. It opens a muted sage-green panel, with a collapse handle on the right and recipe details below the graph.
[*]Use the mouse wheel to zoom, click and drag to pan, and click an item or process to highlight its connections. Single-click selection preserves the current view. Focus and double-click can recenter the selected branch.
[*]Search reads the active game's loaded item catalogue, including native items, mod items, and items with no registered producer. Filters cover Magnum production, workbenches, station industry, and recovery.
[*]The graph shows multi-ingredient recipes, alternative producers, downstream uses, and multiple outputs. The details panel reports ingredients, output quantities, base time, venue, and captured access/readiness information.
[*]Quantity labels now appear only on connections incident to the selected item or process, reducing dense-view overlap while retaining every connection and all detail values. Both lower detail columns have auto-hiding scrollbars and a stable gutter.
[*]Opening the map or pressing Refresh reads current recipe knowledge, cargo, station listings, modified-project outputs, and custom output batches. Occult routes report recipe knowledge and fabrication capability separately, with physical pattern-chip sources where registered.
[*]Explicit recycling routes now show their actual consumed-input batch size. Whole-batch routes explain combined selected stacks and retained remainders instead of presenting their output as a per-item return.
[*]Expand inputs or uses to follow longer chains. Large views expand in pages; unknown workbench quantities and times remain marked as unknown.
[*]Collapse or Escape returns to Production. Leaving Production removes the tab. The map is read-only; it does not place crafting orders.
[/list]

[h1]Crafting balance retained from the recent updates[/h1]
[list]
[*]Existing custom Magnum recipes retain their different ingredient types while each required quantity is halved, rounded up to a whole item with a minimum of one. Existing custom batches are doubled.
[*]The service Lasgun recipe uses one rolled armor plate, one armor fitting, and one weapon component to produce two rifles.
[*]New ordinary component orders generally produce four units; the new ammunition orders produce twelve. Finished medicine, repair, grenade, and cyborg orders produce two.
[*]Production times and normal schematic requirements continue to apply. New occult formulas use the explicit final quantities listed below; the ordinary half-input/double-output adjustment is not applied to them again.
[/list]

SCOPE
The map displays the recipes actually registered by the active game. An item without a producer remains visible as such. This update does not establish a complete raw-material path to every object or full equipment coverage for every otherworldly family; dedicated Olirna stock and remaining family coverage still need work. Quantity planning, automatic queue submission, and complete exports remain further work.

The six capability purchases, independent pattern chips, clone/torment/essence chain, selected otherworldly formulas, and selected Chaos capability gates are implemented in the candidate. Older design documents retain earlier facility proposals; the six-capability implementation supersedes those proposals. The remaining full-family trees and browser planning/export features above remain design work.

The source declares 21 new occult pattern pools and 53 candidate formulas. Actual registered counts depend on installed item definitions and existing recipes; these figures are not a claim that all candidates registered in a running game. Bulk Dirty Corpse Starch recycling is implemented at eight rations per biomass; it does not add a per-item field-disassembly return or a cellulose-pulp conversion.

The startup material-coverage diagnostic examines Magnum and workbench recipes only. It omits Recovery and station routes, as well as quantities and access checks. Its warnings for bone_knife, impaler, and skrivnus_knife do not establish global unreachability: the separately registered Raw Clone recovery route supplies four meat, two skin, and one rib per clone, and those three formulas use ribs. The in-game map includes that route under Recovery when the required native item records exist. This is a source-level route explanation; successful orders and complete self-sufficiency are still unverified.
