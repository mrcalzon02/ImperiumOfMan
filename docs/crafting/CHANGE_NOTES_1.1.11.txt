PREPARED CHANGE NOTES — 1.1.11 CRAFTING MAP AND MATERIALS

[h1]Crafting map[/h1]
[list]
[*]Matched the native menus with the active language font, dark framed panels, uppercase tabs, green and pale-yellow selections, and thin outlined controls. Compact zoom and page buttons receive a text-fit correction.
[*]Separated processing families into clearer branches, with more space between nodes and routed connections. The graph stays inside its own clipped viewport.
[*]Added Ingredient Tree to follow registered recipes, alternative producers and shared ingredients. Recipes added to supported game tables by other mods are included when the map opens or refreshes.
[*]Added recipe icons and distinct artwork for Raw cellulose pulp, Organic oil, Chemical extract, Reactive mineral fraction, Carbon-rich mineral feedstock, Ceramic feedstock and Electronic substrate: seven designs and 21 inventory, floor and shadow sprites.
[*]Kept search, venue filters, pan, zoom, Focus, Fit, branch expansion and recipe details, with the existing Production window ownership and close behaviour.
[/list]

[h1]Two preparation materials[/h1]
[list]
[*]2 Silicate feedstock + 2 Chemical extract make 2 Ceramic feedstock in 2 base hours. One Ceramic feedstock and one Rolled armor plate then make 4 Ceramite plates in 4 base hours.
[*]2 Moldable polymer stock + 2 Acid make 2 Electronic substrate in 2 base hours. One Electronic substrate and one Wire then make 4 Circuit boards in 3 base hours.
[*]Both preparation recipes are shared material recipes. Existing equipment, research and pattern requirements remain in place.
[/list]

Across two assembly orders, material requirements and total base time stay unchanged. Starting with no prepared material, the first four plates take 6 base hours or the first four boards take 5, leaving one spare intermediate for the next order. Production modifiers can affect actual times.

[h1]Ore Cargo supply[/h1]
Added paid Ore Cargo restock targets: 3 at Rachmaninoff, 2 at Cinder Basin Mine and 2 at Foundry Primaris, subject to existing station ownership and Tech 1 requirements. Rachmaninoff and Cinder require CoreWard ownership; Cinder loses this top-up if captured. Foundry Primaris remains Imperial. Station production and trade can consume stock between restocks.

[h1]Finished-ration correction retained[/h1]
Corpse Starch and Dirty Corpse Starch remain finished food with no disassembly return. The removed eight-ration-to-biomass conversion stays removed. Forward manufacturing, eligible spoiled-organic recovery, clone harvesting and equipment salvage remain.
