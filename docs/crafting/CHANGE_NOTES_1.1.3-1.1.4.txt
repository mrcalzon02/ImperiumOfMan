DRAFT CHANGE NOTES — 1.1.3–1.1.4
Native compilation and package verification passed. Game boot and gameplay verification are pending. This is not a Steam release announcement.

[h1]Crafting quantities and production chains[/h1]
[list]
[*]Custom Magnum recipes keep their different ingredient types while reducing each required quantity by half, rounded up to a whole item with a minimum of one.
[*]Existing custom production batches are doubled. For example, one rolled plate, one armor fitting, and one weapon component produce two service Lasguns.
[*]Raw vat biomass can now be separated into cellulose pulp, organic oil, and chemical extract.
[*]Ore now supplies reactive minerals and carbon-rich feedstock alongside refined metal, silicate, and conductive material.
[*]Added connected routes for native acid, gasoline, paper, plastic, rubber, propellant, explosives, and other ordinary crafting inputs.
[*]Added explicit manufacturing routes for mechanical and electrical components, selected medicines, ammunition, repair supplies, and ordinary cyborg parts.
[*]New component orders produce four units; the new ammunition orders produce twelve units. Finished medicine, repair, grenade, and cyborg orders produce two.
[*]Biomass preparation and conductor separation are shared recipes available to new and existing saves. New chips no longer roll those already-known recipes; shared gasoline production is also omitted from new support-chip rolls.
[*]Reclaimed fiber now starts from raw pulp. Polymer stock starts from oil and chemical extract. Polymer scrap can be recycled back into moldable stock.
[*]New medicine schematic pools retain native recipe access. Existing support tiers continue to govern finished ammunition and cyborg recipes. Every custom chip still contains one selected recipe.
[*]Sorbent preserves its existing native production recipe. If that recipe is absent, a carbon-feedstock-and-water fallback is available.
[/list]

[h1]Crafting reference and future expansion design[/h1]
[list]
[*]Added a searchable source recipe map covering 365 explicit Magnum recipes, 303 station records, and 30 conditional native fallbacks. Native-only definitions still require an active-game capture.
[*]Added a raw-material flowchart, an occult-production proposal flowchart, and a reviewed design for a live in-game crafting index.
[*]Documented future occult production: clone stock, torment imprints, suffering essence, Gavvakh, and separate otherworldly material and equipment families. These occult recipes and the new game window are proposals, not implemented features.
[/list]
