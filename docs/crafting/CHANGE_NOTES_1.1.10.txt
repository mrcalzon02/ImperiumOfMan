PREPARED CHANGE NOTES — 1.1.10 RATION RECOVERY CORRECTION

[h1]Keep finished Imperial rations out of recycling[/h1]
[list]
[*]Removed the unintended recipe that turned eight Dirty Corpse Starch rations back into one Raw Vat Biomass.
[*]Corpse Starch and Dirty Corpse Starch remain finished food. Neither ration has a disassembly return.
[*]Removed the dedicated ration batch processing and recycling preview controls that supported that reverse conversion.
[*]Preserved forward ration manufacturing, recovery from eligible spoiled organic items, Raw Clone harvesting, equipment salvage, and protection for locked and quest items.
[*]The Crafting map continues to show the remaining recycling routes with their per-item input and output quantities.
[/list]
