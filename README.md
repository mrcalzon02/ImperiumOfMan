# Imperium of Man

Imperium of Man is a Warhammer 40,000 fan mod that adds the Imperium as a faction in Quasimorph’s campaign. The faction operates Terra, Moon (Luna), Phobos, and Havoc. You can take Imperial contracts, earn faction reputation and equipment, and barter at Imperial stations.

Planetary Defence Force troops patrol in flak armor with Lasguns, and Cadian Guardsmen reinforce them with frag grenades. On Terra, Adeptus Arbites patrols carry Judgement shotguns, while Subductors wield shock mauls. You can fight alongside the Arbites or face them as enemies.

Imperial gear includes Lasguns, Long Las rifles, Bolters, plasma pistols, melta weapons, and armor worn by the Guard and Arbites. Luna stocks Las Clips and Corpse Starch rations, while Phobos produces ammunition and grenades. Imperial Field Plans let you manufacture Las Clips aboard the Magnum.
