# Regional occult ammunition extension

Scope: four further existing native ammo outputs, completing the six-family prototype catalog. All are guarded by installed record and recipe ownership checks; older owned formulas remain compatibility fallbacks when a chain cannot be registered. Existing native and third-party producers, ammo records, drop identities, learned chip IDs and already queued orders remain intact.

Numbers are provisional and exclude feedstock production time. Intermediate facility yield upgrades apply, not finished ammunition. No finished ordinary ammo is consumed by any new active chain. The original Igvas/Duggur batches remain unchanged.

Each new chain has targeted singleton chips for its intermediate and finished recipes plus eligible owned native feedstock formulas. Gannix Angerlings use XiomaraMasks, Ur uses SheduThousand, and the Shartamakhum/Flauros routes use Chaos Undivided as an intermediary, not invented diplomatic factions. Foundation singleton chips are available through these issuers (existing Chaos and Tezctlan keys preserved). The shared seed ledger drives native rewardConsumables registration, paid station restocking and reward observation. Seed stock is one per native feedstock at faction tech 2 (Gannix and Shartamakhum) or 4 (Ur and Flauros), using a valid faction-owned station and no pirate fallback. Existing seed rewards/restock targets are retained. Current station ownership and native faction activation still require runtime verification.

All eight new materials use their existing native donor descriptors provisionally. The crafting map assigns them to the appropriate regional bands. No new native item, weapons, ammo compatibility, or loot behavior is added.

Validation: source-only checks at commit time. Native compile, exact registered recipe counts, contract/trade access, chip learning, queue consumption, completion, save/reload, compatible weapon use and original intermediate art remain outstanding. Prior two prototypes have not received complete gameplay acceptance; this is a source expansion, not a gameplay pass or release.
