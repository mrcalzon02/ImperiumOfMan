Native ship research requires several samples before the corresponding occult production capability exists. Preserve the six rank purchase requirements. Add limited, normal paid stock (top-up to one unit per item on an eligible owned station) and native budget-limited consumable reward registration.

These are additional sample acquisition routes, not free cargo grants or alternate research requirements. Common medical/scientific research equipment retains its native routes. Existing earlier reward entries are preserved. This establishes an intended bootstrap path for active, sufficiently advanced suppliers; eliminated/hostile or non-tradable factions and actual contract eligibility can still constrain access. No claim of guaranteed purchases.

All dedicated occult chip pools now carry issuer metadata, including legacy mixed pools. Ordinary mixed Chaos tier chips are also recorded as knowledge sources for their occult outputs. Dedicated faction pattern markets no longer switch to pirate stock when the maker becomes inactive, except the historical ChurchGannix pool which retains its previous compatibility fallback pending installed-game confirmation of its acquisition route. Existing stocked/learned chips are not deleted. Ordinary mixed industry catalogs retain prior behavior.

Crafting map pattern labels display the intended issuer and faction tech tier. Research-sample items show their acquisition hints. The save-load audit reports active/tradable factions, station ownership, eligible native contract chip counts and actual stock presence; mission audit covers the tracked pools and research samples. These logs do not establish successful trade or payout.

## Acquisition integrity continuation

The recovered disposable campaign had Chaos Undivided and Tezctlan active, but Shedu's Thousand and Xiomara Masks inactive with no owned stations. Mandatory general research specimens now have the alternative issuers listed above: recovered Mars Skin and Martian heads through Chaos at the same faction tech requirements; Quasimorph samples through Tezctlan at tech 1. These are bounded normal trade/reward routes for **specimens only**. Ur/Mars fabrication patterns keep their existing issuer, identity and tier. No automatic recipe learning, free samples, changes to research price, or guaranteed availability are introduced. Native medical/scientific equipment routes remain intact.

The installed native `UnlockDatadisk` code also grants default weapon ammunition or entire matching armor sets for some production chips. The new exact-selection hook is limited to tracked mod chips whose saved selection is an occult recipe. It teaches that output only, preserves native notification/statistic/consumption for a successful new pattern, and preserves a duplicate without learning or consuming it. Normal chips and ordinary legacy rolls retain native behavior. Previously learned patterns are not removed. Research confirmation logs the native counted materials before and after, whether the perk was installed, and any recipes added. These traces are observations of actual player transactions, not synthetic acceptance results.
