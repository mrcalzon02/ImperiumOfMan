# Focused manufacturing intermediates

The existing source already supplies 38 ordinary Industry materials, five chemistry fractions, and mechanical, electrical, medical and automata component chains. The bounded source export does not establish whole-game coverage: native records are not loaded, and raw gatherables, salvage wrecks and mission items retain their acquisition rules.

This addition separates two preparation steps from their existing Magnum assembly recipes. It does not add manufacturing routes for Purity Seals, native bulk cargo or finished-ration recovery.

## Effective recipes

The following quantities are the results after the existing `IndustryRecipe` helper halves declared ingredient counts with upward rounding and doubles the declared batch. Times are base ship-hours before any native production modifiers.

| Recipe | Effective inputs | Output | Base hours |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Ceramic feedstock (`iom_ceramic_feedstock`) | 2 Silicate feedstock + 2 Chemical extract | 2 Ceramic feedstock | 2 |
| Ceramite plates — previous | 1 Rolled armor plate + 1 Silicate feedstock + 1 Chemical extract | 4 native `ceramite_plates` | 5 |
| Ceramite plates — current | 1 Rolled armor plate + 1 Ceramic feedstock | 4 native `ceramite_plates` | 4 |
| Electronic substrate (`iom_electronic_substrate`) | 2 Moldable polymer stock + 2 Acid | 2 Electronic substrate | 2 |
| Circuit boards — previous | 1 Moldable polymer stock + 1 Wire + 1 Acid | 4 native `circuit_board` | 4 |
| Circuit boards — current | 1 Electronic substrate + 1 Wire | 4 native `circuit_board` | 3 |

Each intermediate contains the material cost previously paid directly by one consumer order. Its preparation contributes one base hour per unit. Over two consumer orders, total ingredient quantities and base time are unchanged: ten hours for eight ceramite plates, or eight hours for eight circuit boards.

**First-order latency:** starting with no prepared intermediates requires the complete two-unit preparation batch. The first four ceramite plates therefore require six base hours and leave one Ceramic feedstock; the first four circuit boards require five base hours and leave one Electronic substrate. The next order can consume that spare unit. The five/four-hour totals are amortized values, not first-order completion times.

## Access, order and recovery

Both intermediates use `SharedRecipe`, as their existing consumers already do. The existing new-campaign and save-load paths learn shared material recipes. Finished equipment, native/physical-chip access and occult capabilities retain their existing gates. Station factory recipes remain separate alternative routes.

`RegisterIndustry` calls `RegisterManufacturingIntermediates` after registering the ordinary and chemistry materials, before `RegisterComponentFoundations` registers either consumer. `AddMaterial` creates independent item records and cloned descriptors. Each new material explicitly receives a separate empty `Disassembly` list, and no recycling profile is added for either. Native glass and circuit-board donor records are not changed.

The finished-ration correction and protected recycling source are unchanged. The seven material artwork stems require independent 24×24 inventory, 16×16 floor and 16×16 shadow assets, imported through `tools/export_chemistry_art.py`; generated artwork and native validation are tracked separately.
