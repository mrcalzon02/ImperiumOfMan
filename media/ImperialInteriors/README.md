# Imperial interiors

The Imperial kit depicts the Golden Throne’s outer precinct. The clean floors use honed blue-gray basalt, gunmetal decking, and dark red runners. The wall tops use rough umber masonry, the facades use iron-banded courses, and the pillar caps use a cast bronze surface. The second warp-corruption pass splits slabs, buckles deck plates, and builds deposits of pale mineral and dark roots across several cells.

## Native room presets

New raid rooms come from the installed Quasimorph room pools. The generator adds two or three tiles along each eligible axis, including mixed width and length increases, using seams clear of fixtures and wall junctions. Doors, approaches, facades, filled wall crowns, fixtures, and objectives move with their original sections. Rooms with tight geometry use their original dimensions. Imperial and Chaos Undivided missions choose the corresponding material finish.

Compatible generated raids use three-tile-wide horizontal and vertical spines. Both runs have native doors at each end and at least five side doors leading to separate rooms. L-shaped offshoots turn in any of four orientations. Their continuous three-tile width carries through the bend, with legs of at least 15 tiles and native joints for smaller branches. The generator fits the stock lift, shuttle, and objective rooms into the larger floor alongside the optional rooms. Static maps and specialized layouts keep their own generation rules.

Each branching floor adds a dozen compact supply closets. They open from the sides of straight and L-shaped halls through native doors. Stock boxes, mop buckets, and pickable cleaning supplies sit inside the finished walls with clear access from the door.

The center stripe marks the clear route through each hall. The outer strips hold furnishings, barrels, bins, plants, cartons and cargo, loose salvage, and scattered paper, foil, wire, and packing debris. Small spills and stacks vary from one run to the next. Their footprints leave the center lane, door landings, and full-width bends open. Wall-mounted lights illuminate the runs and a short roller conveyor marks a cargo station.

Perimeter trim follows the inside edge of the walkable floor. Transit rooms, workshops, cargo bays, laboratories, and ceremonial rooms use materials suited to their role. Door approaches and machine service aisles stay open. Floor materials cover connected runs and work bays, with transitions at the edge where the materials meet. Wall surfaces use the original wall topology and the corresponding facade and crown materials.

Floor-to-floor elevators use the native door and cabin animation. Their Imperial palette blends into the wall around the opening. The roof remains part of the wall, and the lift uses the native floor selector for both directions.

Run `python render_tiles.py` with Pillow to regenerate the terrain atlases. The in-game room layouts come from the native generator.

## Wall topology

The installed Military, BramDuggur, and BramShartamakhum wall sheets use an 8 × 6 grid of 28-pixel cells. Quasimorph's corner-wall resolver checks all eight neighbors. A diagonal affects a join only when both bordering cardinal walls are present. The Imperial sheets follow the same slot order, with 47 shapes and the facade at slot 47. The renderer selects caps, straights, inside and outside corners, junctions, and piers from the grid. The stone texture fills the plate to every edge. Exposed edges have a narrow warm rim based on the Military and Duggur palettes, with fixed lighting across every shape.

## Physical damage

The damage atlases cover clean and warp-corrupted materials. Walls and pillar tops use the same 48 topology slots in their intact and chipped stages. A chipped wall blocks movement and a breach leaves passable rubble. Wall faces pass through a ruined stage before becoming rubble. Basalt, decking, chapel marble, and slate each have intact, chipped, and heavily broken tiles. The floor variant follows the cell through each stage.

New floors at Imperial-controlled stations use the Imperial finish. Stations controlled by Chaos Undivided use the warp-corrupted finish of the same tile family. The terrain palette is set when a raid or visit starts and recorded with it. The materials use Quasimorph's terrain transformations and retain the saved health of Imperial-family tiles when a floor is reloaded. Single-cell doors and the standard two-cell vertical doors display damaged closed, damaged open, both stages of shatter, and collapsed art on the matching axis. The native door timers, animations, and collision states drive the transitions.

Run `python render_damage.py` to regenerate the runtime atlases and the [damage preview](imperial-damage-preview.png). These atlases are embedded in the mod assembly on build.

## Production machinery

New floors at selected Imperial and Chaos Undivided stations can place an armor press, caster, construction gantry, las-cell line, vat bank, or hydraulic relay. Each station's roster reflects the materials it handles. The machinery goes in suitable workrooms with a clear service aisle and space around existing doors, props, and exits. The machines use Quasimorph's inventory interaction and each contains one piece of line-specific output or salvage.
