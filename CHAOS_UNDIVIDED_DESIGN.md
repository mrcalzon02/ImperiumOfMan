# Chaos Undivided campaign design

Chaos Undivided has six campaign holdings, a ten-tier supply catalog, eleven enemy forms, and two five-rank skull-pact paths. The host progresses from Furies and corrupted mortals to daemon engines, a Daemon Prince, and Be’lakor’s manifestation at Black Meridian. Tech Levels 1–10 each introduce a weapon, a four-slot armor set, a field supply, a signature salvage component, and a schematic.

Be’lakor’s host gives the faction a 40K precedent for fielding daemons alongside corrupted mortals. The launch roster centers on the Undivided host, with corrupted hosts and Soul Forge engines filling distinct combat roles. The Furies reference comes from Warhammer Legends. The Undivided Fury and Soul Grinder variants use that source material as a starting point for ImperialERA encounters. A warning precedes its sustained burst. Give the floor two approaches and space around the engine so its footprint leaves the exit usable. |
| **9** | **Undivided Daemon Prince.** Hellforged halberd, infernal cannon and heavy warp plate. | The Prince uses direct Chaos fire and can invoke Crown of Ash. Cover or a ward helps the crew survive long enough to counterattack. |
| **10** | **Be’lakor, the Dark Master.** Blade of Shadows, a shadow shroud, and a summoned attack adapted for the encounter. | One manifestation on a dedicated final floor. Breaking two anchors weakens its shroud. Mark the sword sweep before damage resolves and keep a route to break line of sight. |

The Heretic Astartes references cover the Accursed, Spawn, Possessed, Warp Talons, Venomcrawler and Obliterator. The Undivided Daemon Prince uses the published kit’s allegiance option and the Hellforged weapons and infernal cannon as references. Be’lakor’s cloak of shadow and Blade of Shadows drive the finale. Encounter placement, resistances, attacks and rewards are ImperialERA designs. ## Recoverable weapons and armor

Armor pieces use the native head, torso, legs and boots slots, in that order below. Give each set a purpose through its individual pieces so crews can mix gear. A creature’s claws, fused guns, carapace and shroud stay with its combat record. Caches and recovered plans supply wearable gear and portable weapons. All equipment names and protection targets below are proposed for ImperialERA.

## Ten-tier supply catalog

The Imperium issues standardized kit through regimental requisitions. Chaos Undivided outfits its cults and forge crews with gear rebuilt from the breach. Chaos armor trades broad protection for specializations in blunt, shock, pierce, fire, or Chaos damage. Its rations and late-tier treatments can raise local Quasimorphosis. Earlier stock remains in circulation as the faction advances. [Imperial formations progression](IMPERIAL_FORMATIONS_DESIGN.md) · [Imperial supply and production](PRODUCTION_CHAIN_DESIGN.md)

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Hollow Signal Depot** | **Hollow Signal Pattern** | **Hide Binding**, used to make the Fury Fang Knife and Hidewraps and to mend Hidewrap gear in the field. | **Oathbread** provides a compact ration at the cost of Quasimorphosis. Water and rags round out the early supply pool. |

| **8 · Chainforge** | **Chainforge Siege Schematic** | **Harvester Feed Block**, used in the support gun and siege armor. | **Cinder Salve** treats fire and plasma wounds. The Harvester consumes five or eight standard Heavy rounds per burst. |
| **9 · Black Meridian** | **Hellforged Panoply Pattern** | **Infernal Seal**, needed for the Hellforged Blade and panoply. | **Infernal Draught** suppresses pain while raising Quasimorphosis. The blade spends two BatteryCells per powered swing. |

Each Chaos schematic contains one randomly selected Magnum recipe from its tier's supply pool. Like a standard item chip, its selected recipe is fixed on the chip and shown in its tooltip. Further chips can contain other recipes or duplicates; a chip for an already known recipe remains marked as learned. The Hollow Signal pool contains the Fury Fang Knife, the four individual Hidewraps pieces, Oathbread, Hide Binding, and Carrion Mark. The Fury Sigil is outside the fabrication pools. The Tech 10 pool contains the individual Shadowbound armor pieces, Umbral Suture, and Shadow Anchor Fragments. Each regular weapon and armor order uses one signature component plus ordinary material and fittings. Armor comes in individual pieces so crew members can combine protection from different tiers. [Equipment records](src/Plugin.ChaosEquipment.cs) · [Schematic, recipe and reward records](src/Plugin.ChaosProduction.cs)

Procedural raids against a Chaos-held site can recover that site’s eligible chip, salvage, field supply and regular weapon with compatible ammunition. Chaos faction rewards provide another source for eligible earlier tiers, including after an outpost changes hands. Black Meridian can supply Tech 10 armor patterns and fragments through those routes. The Shadowblade comes from the Black Meridian finale. [Raid supply placement](src/Plugin.ChaosCaches.cs)

## Combat and kill payouts

Chaos garrisons use eleven creature profiles. Furies close in packs, Accursed Mutants spit at short range, and Torments punish crews that let them reach melee. Spawn resist blunt damage and knockback. Warp Talons get four base actions and can cross obstacles their flight movement permits. Venomcrawlers pressure the crew with shock bursts, Obliterators place armor-piercing shots at range, and Soul Grinders lay down sustained fire. The Daemon Prince uses direct Chaos fire alongside Crown of Ash. Chaos garrisons stay on the floor during Somnia, so a crew can finish the fight and collect the bounty. The crew can recover portable weapons from caches and contracts.

Difficulty and body effects adjust health in the raid. The base scrip value is before station modifiers. Each hostile kill also drops tier fabrication salvage and either Bound Ash or a Soulforge Ember. The loot stays in the raid until the crew extracts it. Summons, allied creatures, and repeat deaths of the same creature yield no new bounty. [Enemy and bounty records](src/Plugin.ChaosEnemies.cs)

### Black Meridian

At Chaos Tech 10, a rival faction can offer an elimination contract at Black Meridian. Be’lakor appears on the final floor. Two destructible crystals in his chamber each add 12 resistance to his shroud. A violet marker warns the crew one turn before his sweep hits that tile and its immediate surroundings. The sweeps come more often after both anchors fall. He drops Shadowblade, the anchor schematic, fragments, scrip, and the Black Meridian Skull. Taking Shadowblade back to the Magnum records the recovery for the campaign. Later Black Meridian raids continue supplying fragments and high-tier material. ## Skull pacts and rituals

Skulls fit the native pact slot and use the Morphanal ritual system. Upgrades and downgrades follow the current path. A sidegrade crosses to the other pact at the same rank, except that rituals cannot create a Fury Sigil. Bound Ash contributes stability and Soulforge Embers contribute power. The Undivided essence works with existing ritual materials, with agga favoring power and siaira favoring stability. Failed Undivided rituals yield Bound Ash.

The Fury Sigil is purchased from Chaos Undivided from Tech 1. Each station currently owned by the faction stocks one and replenishes it through normal market restocks. Furies drop scrip, salvage, and Bound Ash, but no sigil. The sigil is excluded from fabrication plans, mission rewards, and raid caches; Carrion Mark cannot sidegrade into it and Step Between cannot downgrade into it. An already owned sigil remains usable and can still upgrade or sidegrade into other pacts. [Pact records](src/Plugin.ChaosPacts.cs) · [Faction shop stock](src/Plugin.IndustrySupply.cs)

| Skull rank | Ravage path | Veil path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Carrion Mark** fires a short Chaos bolt. | **Fury Sigil** summons a Fury into a free adjacent cell. |
| **2** | **Rib Cage** grows a protective shell and sends a close shock pulse. | **Step Between** crosses up to four tiles through shadow. |
| **3** | **Sever the Binding** fires a penetrating Chaos lance that can root its target. | **Black Carapace** grants 20 protection for eight turns. |
| **4** | **Excruciator Chain** sends shock projectiles at enemies near the selected cell. | **Soulforge Muster** summons a Venomcrawler within three tiles. |
| **5** | **Crown of Ash** burns a targeted area and can catch the crew in the blast. | **Black Meridian** steps up to six tiles and grants a temporary protective shell. |

Pact use builds the Undivided curse. Its thresholds raise door noise and pain, erode equipment protection, strengthen enemy Chaos damage, and finally cut sight range. Enemy AI can invoke selected offensive and defensive pacts. Player summons inherit the caster’s alliance. [Pact, curse and ritual records](src/Plugin.ChaosPacts.cs)

## Original artwork

The catalog uses original inventory and floor sprites on all 80 supply entries. Ten pact skulls, two ritual materials, and Warp Scrip have their own graphics. The ten held weapons and forty wearable armor pieces use existing Quasimorph donor meshes with new fittings and finishes. The fittings follow each donor’s grip, muzzle, and armor joints. The eleven enemy forms reshape native Quasimorph actor meshes and add role-specific geometry and materials. ## Holdings and regional influence

Give Chaos Undivided six named locations with visible map ownership, ordinary faction contracts and a local garrison. Each is a new station on an existing world or orbit. The faction starts with all six holdings and Tech Level 1. The tiers below are when each site’s signature encounters and rewards become available. Until then, its ordinary contracts use eligible lower-tier troops and equipment.

| System and site | Signature tech | Local role and contracts | Native layout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mars · Phobos — Hollow Signal Depot** | **1–2** | A freight relay turned cult landing point. Raid a breach cache guarded by Furies, then clear the Torment and Mutants from the receiving bay. Its proximity to the default campaign starting point makes it the first playable site. | Orbital spaceport `orbit_Spaceport` |
| **Earth · Luna — Ossuary Works** | **3–4** | A surface reclamation plant supplies rib stock and bindings. Recover materials from the Spawn pens, then take the Binding Icon from a Possessed-led garrison. | Lunar industrial `moon_Industrial_moon` |
| **Venus — Veiled Gate** | **5** | A surface launch post stages Warp Talon raids. Sabotage its departure beacon or recover Talon equipment from the assault locker. | Venus spaceport `venus_Spaceport_venus` |
| **Mercury — Cinder Crucible** | **6–7** | An orbital machine works tests Excruciator weapons and stores fleshmetal. A Venomcrawler guards the forge bay and an Obliterator holds the salvage vault. | Orbital industrial `orbit_Industrial` |
| **Jupiter · Io — Chainforge** | **8** | A surface foundry assembles siege chassis. Sabotage the production line or recover the Harvester plans from the Soul Grinder’s bay. | Io industrial `mercury_Industrial_io` |
| **Saturn · Titan — Black Meridian** | **9–10** | The faction’s fortified command station. Tech 9 opens the Daemon Prince’s command chamber. Tech 10 opens the Be’lakor finale and then repeatable anchor recovery. | Orbital military `orbit_Military` |

The five outposts can change hands through normal station capture. Their power, research and garrisons follow the current owner. Ownership of the depot and launch post provides staging points for nearby raids. The Crucible supplies research and the Chainforge supplies heavy matériel. Begin with modest station contributions and compare the faction’s combined growth and mission frequency with neighboring factions in a campaign test. Keep Black Meridian as the durable Chaos capital so the finale and its follow-up contracts have a consistent home.

Use the existing owner, station type, contract and capture systems. Choose rival sponsors from eligible local factions, using current diplomacy and the native mission limits. Register fresh station IDs and keep ambient Bramfatura rules regional. Route dedicated Chaos breaches and their loot through the new site and mission records. On capture, future station contracts, stock refreshes and production follow the new owner. Use faction reward contracts as a fallback for eligible schematics from lost outposts. ## Campaign integration and validation

1. **Prototype the Phobos depot and its Tech 1–2 breach.** Give the faction its own records, gear category, encounter pools and reward chips. Reuse a native mission layout and the mod’s staged registration pattern. Verify campaign-map ownership, contract generation and the first recovery loop. Check ownership, local contracts and reward pools through capture and save/load. The installed Quasimorph build derives Bramfatura invasion tech from floor depth. Model faction contract tech and invasion floor tech independently, and scope the new roster to Chaos missions. [Station assignments](src/Plugin.Stations.cs) · [Faction unit generation](src/Plugin.Faction.cs)
2. **Add Luna, Venus and Mercury for Tech 3–7.** Use mostly earlier enemies with one new specialist in a suitable room. Prototype Warp Talon insertion with the ordinary pathing grid. Test the Venomcrawler’s footprint, door behavior, collision, targeting and extraction before letting it roam. A fixed forge-bay position is an initial fallback.
3. **Add Io and Titan for Tech 8–10.** Verify the Grinder and Prince silhouettes, melee reach and attack warnings on real generated maps. Keep the Prince rare and put Be’lakor in a unique finale. Check that the anchors visibly change the fight and that the unique reward survives save/load without paying twice. Open repeatable anchor recovery after the finale so a lost armor piece can be replaced.
4. **Play through each tier and one mixed kit.** Confirm first eligible tech, enemy generation, ammo consumption, loot, chip use, fabrication, gear fitting and save/load. Verify all six map entries, contract generation under current ownership and the schematic fallback after capture. Check that earlier roles still appear in late squads, Imperial and native encounters still generate, and capstone rooms always leave a usable route to extraction.

Single-god detachments can extend the faction later with Bloodletters, Horrors, Plaguebearers and Daemonettes as summoned allies. Vashtorr fits a future rival forge encounter that explains access to daemon engines through a bargain with the Soul Forge.
